## Build name

Compliance Map

## Short description

Compare the paperwork for one business activity across several places, with official sources, checked dates and a clear account of what remains unknown.

## What it does

Choosing where to start a business means comparing several different sets of rules. A café needs a different investigation from a home-based software consultancy, and a single fee rarely describes the whole cost of opening.

Compliance Map takes the activity, its scope and at least three places. It puts licences, filings, tax, premises rules, training, scoped costs, timing, renewals and ongoing duties into the same comparison. Each retained requirement links to its official source. The recommendation explains a specific advantage and the unresolved trade-offs so the reader can decide what to investigate next.

## How TinyFish is used

One TinyResearch Deep request researched both demonstration activities across the City of London, New York City and Dublin. The saved run completed on 8 November 2026. A typed parser checked activity/place coverage, official citations, dates and numerical claims.

A separate bounded TinyFish Fetch audit retrieved the exact official pages. Local field reviews then checked meaning, scope and applicability against the saved page text. The reviewed report retains 31 requirements and withholds 2 that their cited sources did not support. The decision layer keeps payment periods, currencies, conditional obligations and alternative filing routes separate.

The local app replays this genuine recorded evidence. Preparing another brief starts no provider job. Fresh research is a separate, disabled-by-default path with one shared reservation, private acceptance receipts and cancellation handling. Replaying evidence is never presented as a new live run.

## The two demonstrations

**A small café.** Compare the same scope in three places. The reviewed recommendation highlights the scoped food-registration charge in the City of London and Dublin, while keeping premises costs, optional charges, timing qualifications and other unknowns visible. It does not claim the lowest overall setup cost.

**A home-based software consultancy.** Switch activity without losing the place comparison. A reviewed rule about the registration sequence gives a reason to begin due diligence with the City of London. Tax, premises, personal circumstances and activity-specific permissions remain separate questions. Unknown rules elsewhere are not treated as prohibitions.

## Limits

The report supports a scoped starting point for further checks. Complete setup totals, end-to-end approval durations, address-specific suitability and legal completeness remain unestablished. Unselected filing alternatives are not summed together, missing amounts are not zero, and notice or processing milestones are not guaranteed opening dates.

The source audit fetched 35 pages. Its 36th request returned a response that the client could not validate, so the shared pool stopped. That source remains uncorroborated, and no automatic retry followed. The original Research result and intermediate reports remain preserved separately from the local review.
